CID resilience

Paste a CID. The page finds who advertises it, fetches random blocks from each provider, verifies the hashes, and times each fetch against that provider's own no-op baseline to tell a local copy from one fetched on demand. Runs entirely in your browser. What it measures and what it can't.

Rounded to 0.1 degree (~11 km). Self-reported: anyone merging your report trusts it.
